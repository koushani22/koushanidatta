Behind the work

About me

Hi, I’m Koushani (Kos). A branding & packaging designer who builds brands people want to pick up.

I design identities and packs that hold up from the first sketch to the shelf, and bring them to life in 3D and motion. Bronze at Pentawards 2026. MA Graphic Branding & Identity at UAL and a Graduate Diploma from the Royal College of Art, after five years as an electrical engineer with Indian Railways. That’s where I learned to think in systems, and I still design that way.
