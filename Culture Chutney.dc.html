Case study

A bubbling pot of culture.

RoleBrand identity, illustration and visual communication
DeliverablesBrand identity, illustrations, social media, signage, bags, event danglers, tickets
01The market

Culture Chutney is an eco-conscious flea market bringing independent makers, artists and communities together across cities in India. I developed its brand identity, illustrations and communication across social media and event materials.

02Identity

Rounded lettering and flowing forms give the identity a playful, welcoming character. Green anchors the palette, connecting the visual language to the market’s focus on sustainability.

03The idea

A bubbling pot becomes a gathering place for everything the market holds. Clothing, plants, jewellery and handmade objects spill into the composition, a visual mix of culture and creativity. In collaboration with SOCIAL, the market travels between SOCIAL venues across India.

04Venues and makers

Each venue becomes part of the story. Hand-drawn architecture, local landmarks and everyday street scenes give the editions their own character while keeping the identity recognisable. The illustrations also make room for individual makers. Their products become part of the setting, carrying the same visual language through announcements, artist features and market line-ups.
