RoleFreelance brand designer · Solo project
DeliverablesIdentity, illustration, signage, tickets, merch, social
01Challenge

Brand an eco-conscious flea market of food, craft and culture.

02Insight

A market runs on atmosphere, so the brand has to live on signage, tickets and totes, not just a logo.

03Idea

A bubbling pot of culture: hand-drawn illustration and a bold custom wordmark.

04Outcome

Signage, tickets, danglers, tote bags and social, delivered for the live event.

The brand A pot of culture, stirred.

An eco-conscious flea market celebrating diverse cultural artistry, giving small makers a platform to sell handmade, sustainable work through a vibrant, community-driven identity.
