A stackable spirit, where every bottle is a piece of art.

RoleBrand & packaging designer · Solo project
DeliverablesNaming, identity, structural packaging, 3D, campaign
01Challenge

Design a spirit for people who move cities and rent their walls.

02Insight

Bottles bought on the move clutter a shelf, then get thrown away.

03Idea

A stackable stoneware cube, with each series drawn by a different artist.

04Outcome

A bottle that outlives the drink: refillable, collectable and stackable into your own display.
