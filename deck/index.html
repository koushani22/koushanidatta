04
Case study

Takeaway packaging grown from fungi, chosen in one tap.

RoleBrand & packaging designer, with Sarah Kai, Sayaka Miyashita, Viktoriia Kryvorotenko and Cibelle
DeliverablesMaterial story, packaging range, delivery-app integration
01Challenge

Rethink takeaway packaging for a plastic-free delivery future.

02Insight

People want greener packaging, but won’t go out of their way to choose it.

03Idea

Make mycelium the default: a one-tap packaging option inside the delivery apps people already use.

04Outcome

A packaging range, identity and an Uber Eats ordering flow with a WWF donation toggle.

Brand story

The future is mycelium made.

Mycelium is the root network of fungi, the stuff most people see as mould, rot and dirt. Myceliyum shows it is one of the most useful materials we have.

Grown on farm waste, it is moulded into takeaway boxes in days, holds a hot meal, and goes back to the soil in about six weeks. Plastic stays for centuries.

From nature, for the future.

Lethea

Functional Darjeeling tea for the rhythm of the day.
02
Case study

Darjeeling tea, rebuilt as a daily ritual: one for the morning, one for the night.

RoleResearch, naming, wordmark, illustration, packaging system, campaign, retail & testing
ContextMA Graphic Branding & Identity, UAL London College of Communication, 2026
01Challenge

Darjeeling’s origin is legally protected, but the people who pick it are invisible at the shelf, and wellness brands sell vague functional promises.

02Insight

People already use tea to switch on and switch off. The ritual exists; it just needs a system.

03Idea

START (First Flush + Mandarin, 90 mg caffeine) for after waking; RELEASE (Second Flush + Plum, decaf, 54 mg L-theanine) for before sleep. Every pack traces its estate through topographic stamps.

04Outcome

Naming, custom wordmark, picker illustration, stamp system, gummies, cold brew, 20 ml sachets, capsules, cartons, campaign and retail. A/B tested with real participants.

The story

Some things should be passed down. Others shouldn’t.

Darjeeling’s name is protected by law. The people who pick it are not. Tea knowledge moves through generations, and so do the labour structures that keep value away from them.

Lethea turns that tension into a daily ritual: START for the morning, RELEASE for the night. Every pack traces its estate, its elevation and its flush, so the origin you taste is the origin you can see.

Flavours

Mandarin by day. Plum by night.

First Flush for the morning ritual, Second Flush for the evening.

Capsules & concentrate

The ritual, in a single drop.

Darjeeling tea concentrate sealed in soft capsules: amber START for the morning, violet RELEASE for the night. Same estate stamps, same doses, no brewing needed.

Sachets

One sachet. One ritual.

Tear, pour, dilute — a 20 ml dose for each end of the day.

Stamp system

Every pack traces its estate.

87 garden stamps drawn from real elevation data, plus dose, flush and compound stamps that sit on every pack.
